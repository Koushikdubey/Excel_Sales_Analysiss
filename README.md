# Sales Performance Analysis Dashboard | Advanced Excel

## Project Overview
Developed an interactive sales performance analysis dashboard using Microsoft Excel to analyze retail sales data from 2022–2024. The project focuses on identifying revenue trends, evaluating product and regional performance, comparing sales channels, and examining promotional activity to support data-driven business decisions.

## Tools & Technologies
- Microsoft Excel
- Power Query for data cleaning and transformation
- PivotTables and PivotCharts
- Excel formulas and calculated columns
- Slicers and interactive KPI dashboard
- Excel Copilot (if used)

## Key Analysis Areas
- **Sales Trends:** Monthly and yearly revenue and units sold.
- **Category & Brand Performance:** Revenue comparisons and top-performing products.
- **Regional Analysis:** Sales contribution across regions.
- **Channel Analysis:** Revenue distribution across Retail, E-commerce, and Discount channels.
- **Promotional Analysis:** Comparison of promotional and non-promotional sales.
- **SKU Performance:** Product-level revenue and sales volume analysis.

## Key KPIs
- Total Revenue
- Total Units Sold
- Sales Growth %
- Revenue by Category, Brand, Region, and Channel
- Promotional vs. Non-Promotional Revenue

## Business Insights
- Identified leading product categories and brands by revenue.
- Evaluated regional and sales-channel contributions.
- Analyzed monthly sales trends and year-over-year growth.
- Examined promotional performance to identify opportunities for improvement.
- Developed recommendations for product assortment, inventory availability, delivery performance, and targeted promotions.

## Deliverables
- Interactive Excel sales dashboard
- Data cleaning and transformation workflow
- PivotTable-based analytical reports
- Business insights and recommendations
- Supporting case-study documentation
